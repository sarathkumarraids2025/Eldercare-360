Platform Architecture

Empowering Independent Senior Living Through Intelligent Health Technology

ElderCare 360 is a production-ready, intelligent healthcare management platform engineered to solve the complex daily challenges of geriatric health maintenance, polypharmacy medication management, and proactive multi-stakeholder care coordination.

Supported Multi-Stakeholder Ecosystem

1. Senior Citizens

Simplified high-contrast cards, large clickable buttons, clear food instructions, and audible dosing alarms for maximum independence.

2. Family Guardians

Dedicated family portal offering real-time peace of mind: adherence rates, nurse check-ins, upcoming doctor visits, and instant SOS alerts.

3. Professional Caregivers

Dedicated tools for nurses and home aides to record vitals, verify dose administration, escalate missed doses, and write clinical notes.

4. Physicians & Specialists

Prescription management, 14-day vital signs trajectories, longitudinal charts, and consultation appointment scheduling.

5. System Administrators

Role-based access governance, database schema maintenance, audit trails, and healthcare telemetry supervision.

6. AI Clinical Engine

Random Forest ML model predicting adherence risks, flagging vitals threshold deviations, and outputting actionable clinical guidance.

Medical Assistance Disclaimer

ElderCare 360 is an intelligent monitoring and workflow assistance system. It is designed to assist elderly individuals, caregivers, and families in tracking daily medications and physiological measurements. It does NOT provide autonomous medical diagnoses or prescribe pharmaceutical regimens. All medical advice and therapy changes should be conducted under the direct supervision of a licensed healthcare physician.
